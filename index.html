08Questions

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The calculator, three themes, the sounds and the quick discounts are free, with no ads. Premium adds nine themes, the full history and the GST tools.

Does it need the internet?

No. Sums work offline. The internet is only used to show prices, to buy or restore Premium, and to send anonymous statistics and crash reports.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8 and later. Slower phones get a lighter 3D mode automatically.

Can I try a Premium theme first?

Yes. Tap any theme in the app to see it on your calculator. Unlock Premium to keep it.
