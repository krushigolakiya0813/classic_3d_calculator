Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Your calculations never leave your phone. The app has no ads, needs no account, and we never sell your data. We use a few services to sell premium themes and to find crashes; this page lists them.

Who we are

Classic Calc: 3D Calculator (“the app”) is made by Krushi Technolab (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Android app and this website. If you have a question, write to krushigolakiya0813@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Calculations and results. Everything you type is worked out on your phone. We never see it and it is never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Your theme, number colour, sound, touch-tone, haptics and language are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Clipboard. When you hold the answer, the app copies it to your clipboard. The app never reads your clipboard.

Purchases

Premium themes are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

To check and restore your purchases we use RevenueCat. When you open the store screen or buy something, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID it creates for your copy of the app (not your name or email)
  • what you bought, the purchase token, date, price and currency
  • your store country, the app version, your Android version and device model

See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes.

Firebase Analytics

Collects usage events such as app opens, session length, and which features and themes are used, with the device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country (from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID.

Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the code the crash happened, the device model, Android version, free memory and storage, and a random installation ID. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

None of this includes your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or your calculations. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don’t access your contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state, to load prices, make and restore purchases, and send usage statistics and crash reports.
  • Google Play billing, to buy premium themes.

Who we share data with

Only the services named above (Google Play, RevenueCat and Firebase), which process it for us, and anyone we are required to share it with by law. Data sent to these services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

Settings stay on your phone until you clear them. Crash reports are kept for 90 days, and usage statistics for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and to meet tax and accounting rules.

Your choices and rights

You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes everything stored on your phone. Depending on where you live (for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Because we don’t collect your name or email, we usually cannot tell which data is yours; write to us and we will do what we can, including asking our service providers to delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
