Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Your calculations never leave your phone. The app has no ads, needs no account, and we never sell your data. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and uses a service to sell Premium; this page lists exactly what each one receives.

Who we are

Classic Calc: 3D Calculator (“the app”) is made by Krushi Technolab (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Android app and this website. If you have a question, write to krushigolakiya0813@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Calculations and results. Everything you type is worked out on your phone. We never see it and it is never sent anywhere.
  • History. The app keeps your last 100 calculations, and the sum you were working on when you left, in its private storage on your phone. You can delete one calculation or clear them all in the history. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your settings. Your theme, number colour, whether the app icon follows the theme, sound, touch-tone, haptics and language are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone.
  • Clipboard. Copy puts the answer on your clipboard. The app reads your clipboard only when you open the menu on the display, to see whether there is text to paste, and when you choose Paste, to take the number out of it. What it reads stays on your phone.
  • Sharing. When you share an answer or your history, the app makes a picture or text of it on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. We don’t receive it.
  • Your country. To decide whether to show the GST buttons, the app checks on your phone the country of your mobile network, your SIM and your language setting. This check is not sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensor. The app reads your phone’s gyroscope so the big number moves when you turn the phone. The readings are used on the spot and are not stored or sent.
  • Speed check. On first start the app measures how fast your phone draws the 3D scene, to pick a drawing quality, and keeps the result on your phone.

Everything in this list is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Purchases

Premium (more themes, your full history, the GST split and a rate of your own) is sold through Google Play, as a one-time purchase or a subscription, whichever the app offers you. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

To check and restore your purchases we use RevenueCat. It is set up when the app starts, so a purchase is recognised straight away. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID it creates for your copy of the app (not your name or email)
  • what you bought, the purchase token, date, price and currency
  • your store country, the app version, your Android version and device model

See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes.

Firebase Analytics

Collects usage events such as app opens, session length and screens, with the device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country (from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID. On top of that the app itself reports only these:

  • which theme you chose
  • that the Premium screen was opened, and from where (settings, a locked theme, the history or the quick buttons)
  • that a purchase or a restore was started and how it ended (success, cancelled, pending or the kind of error), with the kind of plan

The app does not use the advertising ID: it is removed from the app, and ad personalisation is switched off.

Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the code the crash happened, the device model, Android version, free memory and storage, and a random installation ID. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

None of this includes your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or your calculations. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don’t use your advertising ID or build an advertising profile of you.
  • We don’t access your contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state, to load prices, make and restore purchases, and send usage statistics and crash reports.
  • Google Play billing, to buy Premium.

Who we share data with

Only the services named above (Google Play, RevenueCat and Firebase), which process it for us, and anyone we are required to share it with by law. Data sent to these services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

Your history and settings stay on your phone until you clear them. Crash reports are kept for 90 days, and usage statistics for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and to meet tax and accounting rules.

Your choices and rights

You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes everything stored on your phone. Depending on where you live (for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Because we don’t collect your name or email, we usually cannot tell which data is yours; write to us and we will do what we can, including asking our service providers to delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
